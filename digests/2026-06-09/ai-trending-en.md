**The dominant signal of 2026-06-09 is the explosive demand for agentic "web intelligence" skills.** The +3,558 single-day stars for `last30days-skill` dwarf all other trending repos combined, revealing a developer community that is past building agent frameworks (the "solved" layer) and is now obsessed with *feeding agents high-quality synthesized knowledge*. Three structural shifts are visible:

1. **Memory as a competitive frontier.** A new wave of agent memory projects surfaced today — `MemPalace`, `cognee`, `claude-emm`, `Perso_AI_Infrastructure` — indicating that the open-source community has identified persistent, cross-session memory as the next critical bottleneck for agentic systems. This aligns with industrywide moves from Anthropic and OpenAI to extend agent context windows with persistent memory stores.

2. **GPU/edge vector infrastructure re-accelerating.** `turbovec (+1,729)` chose Rust for its vector index, and `LEANN` (MLsys'26) showcases 97% storage-efficient private RAG on-device. Together, they signal that the community is rethinking not just *where* vectors are stored but *how efficiently* — driven by hardware constraints on edge devices and the rising cost of cloud vector DBs as data scales.

3. **Platform-level skill ecosystems consolidating.** Google's official launch of `Agent Skills` (+461) into its product stack, paired with NousResearch's Hermes ecosystem (187K stars) and the PM Skills marketplace (100+ skills), shows the industry converging on a "skill protocol" pattern analogous to the MCP protocol explosion of late 2025. The agent stack is now explicitly three layers: **LLM runtime → skill/mcp protocol → application UI**, and every layer has a dominant open-source contender.

The wild card is `career-ops` (50K stars, +308 today) — an AI job-search system purpose-built on Claude Code's agentic harness.
